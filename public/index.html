IR Retrieval Subsystems

Core Operational

Operational pipeline specifications powering the information retrieval system.

Positional Inverted Index

Maps normalized vocabulary words to document IDs with exact word offset arrays for phrase lookups.

Permuterm Wildcard Index

Appends $ sentinel and rotates words to support trailing (comput*), leading (*tion), and middle (te?t) wildcards.

TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity Ranking

Vector Space Model using logarithmic Term Frequency and smoothed Inverse Document Frequency relevance scores.

Boolean Shunting-Yard Evaluator

Converts infix expressions with AND, OR, NOT, and parentheses into RPN for set operations.
